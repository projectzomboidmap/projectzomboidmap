# Project Zomboid Map

I keep a free interactive map of Build 42's Knox Country and a database of
every item in the game — every street, every floor, every axe.

## The map

The full B42 world in a browser tab. Zoom from the whole county down to a
single room. Open any building floor by floor, from the basement to the top
storey, and check the stairwells and roof access before you move in. Plan a
route between any two points and get the distance plus walking and driving
times before your character learns them the hard way. Turn on the zombie
density layer to see where the population is thick and where a street is
quiet enough to loot. Found coordinates in a guide? Type them in and land
on the spot.

Coverage is the complete Build 42 map: Louisville and its suburbs, West
Point, Muldraugh, Rosewood, Riverside, Brandenburg, Ekron, Doe Valley —
and every farm, cabin and gas station between them.

## The item database

5,092 item pages: stats, spawn locations, and recipes in both directions —
what an item crafts, and what crafts it. Start from the thing you need (an
axe, a sledgehammer, the generator magazine) and jump straight onto the map
at the places that stock it.

## Knox Country Base Atlas

The best base locations in Build 42, rated from the map's own data instead
of opinion. Zombie pressure around each base is measured, not guessed: two
aligned renders of the same view, the population layer off and on, compared
pixel by pixel. Loot, water and defences are scored from the world and the
item database, and every score can be reproduced with the script published
in the atlas repository here on GitHub. The first five locations are scored
and published; the list grows to fifty in the open.

## Knox Country Safehouse Jam

A building jam I host on itch.io: build the safehouse you'd trust with your
life, no bigger than 100×100 tiles. Submissions run 23 November to
13 December, public voting follows, and the top three get featured with
screenshots and creator credit.
